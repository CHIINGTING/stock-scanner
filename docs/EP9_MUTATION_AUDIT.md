6. writes the original bytes back and re-checks the SHA-256 against the baseline (a mismatch aborts).

- **M7c collapses the two ambiguity bounds.** EP-9 reports a conservative and an optimistic stop-first rate rather than a point estimate, because daily OHLC cannot order a stop and a target inside one bar.
